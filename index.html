Data honesty

This tracker mixes three tiers of data, tagged on every section so it's never ambiguous which is which:

  • Live — Section 1 only. Pulled automatically from the St. Louis Fed's public FRED database (series DLTRUCKSSAAR, FLTRUCKSSAAR, ALTSALES) by a GitHub Actions job that runs monthly and appends new observations to data/fred-series.json. It is never overwritten — the full pull history is versioned in git.
  • Real — Sections 2–4. Structured numbers entered by hand into data/manual-data.json after being sourced from OEM investor-relations releases, GPI's own 10-Q/10-K filings and earnings calls, or Cox Automotive/KBB press releases (via AlphaSense). No figure in these sections is estimated or interpolated — an empty chart with "awaiting data" means exactly that.
  • Tracked — Sections 5–8. Qualitative status notes maintained by hand, with no numeric series backing them. These reflect the maintainer's read of company disclosures at a point in time, not a live feed.

Nothing manual is ever styled or labeled to look like a live feed. See data/manual-data.json for edit instructions and full source citations per data point.
