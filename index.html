Data honesty

This tracker mixes three tiers of data, tagged on every section so it's never ambiguous which is which:

  • Live — Sections 1–3. Pulled automatically from the St. Louis Fed's public FRED database (series DLTRUCKSSAAR, FLTRUCKSSAAR, ALTSALES, TERMCBAUTO48NS, GASREGW, TXUR, CUSR0000SETA02, CUSR0000SETA01) by a GitHub Actions job that runs monthly and appends new observations to data/fred-series.json. It is never overwritten — the full pull history is versioned in git.
  • Real — Sections 4–8, plus Section 13. Structured numbers entered by hand into data/manual-data.json after being sourced from OEM investor-relations releases, GPI's own 10-Q/10-K filings and earnings calls, Cox Automotive/Manheim/KBB reports, or trade-press coverage where the primary table is not machine-readable (flagged per row). No figure in these sections is estimated or interpolated; derived figures (e.g. Ford Q1 by subtraction, the retail-minus-wholesale spread, used share of revenue) are labeled as derived. Section 6 and 8 charts are drawn from hand-entered data, not a live feed.
  • Tracked — Sections 9–12. Qualitative status notes maintained by hand, with no numeric series backing them. These reflect the maintainer's read of company disclosures at a point in time, not a live feed.

Section 13 (Parts & Service growth) sits below a visual divider and is deliberately outside the vehicle-volume narrative of Sections 1–12 — it's adjacent context for a separate model assumption, not a demand signal.

Nothing manual is ever styled or labeled to look like a live feed. See data/manual-data.json for edit instructions and full source citations per data point.
